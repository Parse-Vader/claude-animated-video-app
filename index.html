claude-animated-video

A private publishing tool for our own YouTube channels. We make animated explainer videos from code (Remotion and Blender) with a synthetic voice, and this tool uploads them to our own channels: one video and one Short per episode, with title, description, tags, chapters and a thumbnail.

The tool is used only by the channel owner. It does not offer sign-in to other people, does not upload third-party content and does not read data from other YouTube users.
