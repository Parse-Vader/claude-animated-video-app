Privacy policy

claude-animated-video · last updated 8 November 2026

Who uses this app

Only the owner of our own YouTube channels. The app is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the Google OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube the app uploads our own videos and Shorts, sets their title, description, tags, thumbnail and visibility, and adds them to our own playlists. It reads only the metadata of the videos it uploaded itself.

What is stored, and where

The OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file outside any source repository. Nothing is sent to a server of ours or to any third party. The app does not collect, store or share data about other YouTube users or viewers.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file removes the stored tokens.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
